How subliminal affirmation software works

An affirmation is a short positive statement, like "I am focused" or "I stay calm under pressure." Affirmation software repeats statements like these automatically, so you don't have to remember to read them yourself. Mindzoom does this in three ways, as the vendor describes them:

  • On-screen messages: text affirmations are shown on screen at high speed, at a position, font and colour you choose.
  • Silent Subliminal Messaging: affirmations are turned into a high-frequency audio signal that you can't consciously hear.
  • Subliminal Mixer: you mix text or recorded affirmations with your own music or nature sounds and save the result as an audio file.

Note: Scientific research on subliminal messages is limited and its results are mixed. Think of affirmation software as a habit and motivation tool, not a treatment. Results vary from person to person and nothing is guaranteed. It does not replace professional medical or psychological advice.
